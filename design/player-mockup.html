Media3Player — UI mockup

Interactive mockup of the custom player UI. Tap the video to show or hide controls, double-tap the sides to seek, hold for 2× speed, and drag up or down on the left or right half of the fullscreen player for brightness and volume.

State
Accent

Gestures

  • Tap: show / hide controls (auto-hide after 3s)
  • Double-tap left / right: seek −10s / +10s
  • Hold: 2× speed while pressed
  • Swipe up/down left half: brightness, right half: volume

Settings sheet

  • Quality (Auto + available video tracks)
  • Playback speed 0.25× – 2×
  • Subtitles and audio tracks
  • Zoom to fill, loop

States

  • Buffering spinner, error with retry
  • Ended: replay + up next with countdown
  • Locked: hides controls to avoid accidental touches
  • Accent color and seek step are block properties

Native implementation

  • Media3 PlayerView for the video surface and captions
  • Custom controls built from Android Views in Kotlin
  • No XML layouts or Compose needed
